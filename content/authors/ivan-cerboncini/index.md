Ivan de Campos Cerboncini is a master's student in the Skaf Lab at the Institute of Chemistry, Unicamp.

His research uses molecular dynamics simulations and molecular solvation theories to investigate cellulose nanofibrillation in different solvents and solvent mixtures, comparing their effectiveness.

[FAPESP research project](https://bv.fapesp.br/pt/bolsas/225741/modelagem-molecular-da-nanofibrilacao-de-celulose-em-diferentes-solventes/)

Thiago's doctoral research focused on cellulolytic enzyme dynamics and cellulose structure. He developed **[Cellulose-Builder](https://ftp.iqm.unicamp.br/pub/cellulose-builder)**, a widely-used toolkit for building crystalline cellulose structures for molecular dynamics simulations, published in the *Journal of Computational Chemistry* (2012).

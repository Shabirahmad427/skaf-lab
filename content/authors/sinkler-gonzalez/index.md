Sinkler Eduardo Tormet Gonzalez is a former PhD student in the Skaf Lab at the Institute of Chemistry, Unicamp. His doctoral research used molecular dynamics simulations to study enzymes involved in cellulosic biomass conversion and glycan saccharification.

His work focused on how enzymes bind cellulose at the atomic level and catalyze the hydrolysis of polysaccharides, including studies of cellobiohydrolases, endoglucanases, and other fungal and bacterial enzymes.

His doctoral thesis, *Molecular dynamics of enzymes for glycan saccharification*, is listed by FAPESP as a 2024 Unicamp thesis. His doctoral fellowship (FAPESP grant 2019/17371-8), supervised by Prof. Munir Skaf, ran from September 2019 to October 2023.

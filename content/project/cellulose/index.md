Brazil is a world leader in sugarcane-based bioenergy. Our group contributes computationally
to the understanding of **enzymatic biomass degradation** — a key step in second-generation biofuel production.

## Research Focus

- Molecular simulations of cellulases and hemicellulases acting on cellulose
- Structural characterization of cellulose polymorphs
- Mechanism of cellulose chain decrystallization and processive degradation
- Interaction of accessory proteins (LPMOs, CBMs) with cellulose surfaces

## Tool

We developed **[Cellulose-Builder](https://ftp.iqm.unicamp.br/pub/cellulose-builder)** —
a toolkit for building crystalline structures of cellulose for MD simulations,
published in the *Journal of Computational Chemistry*.
